# Create

Create is the easy way to make things for a game in Otto: people, objects,
voices, and (soon) sounds and music. You say what you want in your own words.
Otto works out the rest.

You find it in the sidebar under **Product**. It is the first tab, and Product
opens on it. It looks like every other Product page: the sidebar stays, the
Product tabs (Create, Stories, Designs, Voice, Learnings) sit at the top, and
**My creations** is the button on the right of that bar. Inside Create, **Back**
under the bar takes you one step back.

## Make something by describing it

1. Type what you want in the big box, the way you'd tell a friend. For example:
   *"A man pulls a sword out of the ground and fights a monster that guards a
   chest of gold. The key hangs on the monster's neck."*
   You can also tap one of the ideas under the box.
2. Press **Make it**.
3. Otto thinks about it (usually about a minute; a clock shows how long it has
   been, and after two minutes it says plainly that it is taking longer than
   usual and that **Back** keeps it going) and shows **its plan**: a short list of the
   people, things, voices and sounds it will make, each with a picture and one
   sentence.
   - **Not yet** says, once, what can't be made today (for example, people can
     stand, walk and run but can't fight yet).
   - The plan says how long it will take, before you press anything.
   - Want something different? Say so in the box under the plan ("don't make
     the key", "make the monster bigger") and press **Change it**.
4. Press **Make it**. The top says what is being made now, how many pieces
   are ready out of how many, and one time left that counts down. Each piece
   says how it's going:
   - its **picture** as soon as one is drawn, then a picture of the made piece;
   - the step in plain words and which step of how many ("Building the 3D
     shape… · Step 2 of 5"), a clock that ticks ("2:41") and a bar that keeps
     moving during a long step (it never goes back, and never reaches the end
     before the step does);
   - "Waiting for its turn" with its clock when the maker is busy with
     something else;
   - **Ready · Look or change it** when it is made: press the row to look at
     it (a person shows its moves and skeleton, see below). The rest keeps
     going while you look; a note you write there waits for you in the change
     box when everything is made.
   As soon as some pieces stand in the scene, **See the scene so far** shows
   them together, with what is still coming listed beside it.
   You can leave the page: the work keeps going, and the page picks it up when
   you come back. **Stop** stops it; what was already finished is kept.
5. When it's done you see **one scene** with everything placed together. Drag
   to turn it around. A piece that couldn't be made is named under the picture. The voices, sounds and music are listed next to it with
   play buttons. *Play them all together* plays the background sounds and the
   music at once.
6. **Save** puts every piece into your game's folder (the workspace folder,
   under `assets/<the scene's name>/`).

### Several at once

You can start another creation while one is being made. It takes its turn
(Create says so before you press anything), and **Back** never stops or loses
one. Above the big box, a quiet **In progress** list shows each one with its
picture and where it is: *Planning*, *Waiting, 1 ahead*, *Making 3 of 15*, or
*Ready to look* (until you open it). Tap one to open it. **My creations** shows
them too (from every workspace), and the other Product tabs keep working while
things are made. Opening one never changes the workspace the rest of the app has
open.

### Who plans it

Beside **Use a picture**, under the big box, a small chip says which AI agent
and model plan your scene, for example *Claude · Default* (your workspace's
usual agent with its usual model). Tap it to pick another one from the same
list Threads uses, and choose how hard it thinks (**Reasoning**: Low, Medium,
High…) the same way as in Threads. The chip names it when it isn't the default
(*Claude Code · Opus 5.5 · High*). Create remembers your choice for this
workspace. A scene
keeps the agent it was planned with: every change you ask for later uses it
too. It only picks who thinks; the 3D maker is the same.

## Change things (steering)

Changing works the same way as describing:

- Type what you want different in **Want something different? Tell me.**:
  "make the monster bigger", "more gold", "he should sound older".
- Or tap something in the scene. A numbered pin appears; write what should
  change there.
- Press **Change it**. Otto answers in one sentence ("I'll make the monster
  bigger…"), lists your notes, and says how long it takes.
- Press **Make the changes**. Only those pieces are made again.
- If a piece really came out different, you see it **before and after**. Choose
  **Keep the new one** or **Keep the old one**.

## Make just one thing

Under the big box, tap **Choose what to make myself**, then pick:

- **Character**: someone who can move and talk. Describe them, or use a picture.
  **Make it** gives you the whole character: the shape, ready for games, with a
  way to move and basic moves (stand, walk and run). It takes about six
  minutes.
- **Thing**: like a sword or a chest. About five minutes.
- **Voice**: choose who is talking, how they sound (*Deep and calm*, *Young and
  cheerful*, *Soft and husky*, *Rough storyteller*) and type what they say, one
  line per row. **Make them talk** turns each line into a speech bubble you can
  play, change or say again.
- **Sound & music**: say where we are ("a windy cave") and what we should hear
  ("footsteps on stones"), choose a sound or music, and **Make it**. Each one
  plays right there; a background sound can repeat.

The result page shows the thing big. You can:

- **Moves** (for characters): the moves the model really carries as buttons
  (Stand, Walk, Run…). The first one plays as soon as it opens; tap the playing
  one to stop it. It says *Skeleton added · 3 moves*, and **Show skeleton**
  beside the moves draws its bones over it, following the move. The first time
  you open a person with a new skeleton, the skeleton shows for a moment by
  itself. A person whose moves are still being added, or whose moves didn't
  work, says so instead of standing there silently. The same words show on its
  row while making, under the scene, and in My creations.
- **Give it a voice** (for characters).
- **Change something**: the same box and **Change it** button. Tap the model
  where something should change and write what you want (as many notes as you
  like). Otto says what it will change, then **Make the changes** makes a new
  one, shown next to the old one with **Keep the new one** / **Keep the old one**.
- **Save**: saves it into your game's folder.
- **Start over**.

**My creations** (top right) shows everything you've made, newest first, whatever
workspace the rest of the app has open: creations are yours, not a workspace's.
The ones still being made come first and keep up by themselves while the page is
open. It only says *Nothing made yet* when there really is nothing; if the list
can't be loaded it says so, with **Try again**.

## What it can and can't do today

- **People and things** need the maker (Otto Forge) on this Mac. If it isn't
  there, Create says so in one friendly message with a way back. Ask whoever set
  up Otto to install it.
- **Changing a model** makes it again from your idea plus your notes. Otto
  can't yet edit a finished model in place, so the new one can look a bit
  different. Your old one is always kept. A thing made from a picture is
  remade from words you give it.
- **Voices** work right away with the Mac's own voices. With the Otto Forge
  voice server running, the plain voice choices use its character voices.
- **Sounds and music** need the sound maker (Otto Forge's audio stage). Without
  it, Create says "Sounds aren't ready yet" and makes everything else; in a
  scene plan they are listed once under **Not yet**.
- **Describing a whole scene** (Smart mode) needs Otto's planner. On a build
  without it, Create tells you and sends you to *Choose what to make myself*.
- **Moves**: people can stand, walk and run. Other moves (like fighting) are
  listed under **Not yet**.
- **Detail**: everything is made with computer-game detail. Experts can change
  that under **More options**.

## For experts

Every result has a quiet **More options** link that opens the same thing in the
3D Studio or the Voice Studio, with every setting. The technical side (engine
stages, detail levels, voices by name) is described in
[3D Studio, Voice Studio and Otto Forge](otto-forge.md).
