A real isolated score-only evaluation rated 5→1 displayed `1/5` in the rating row and `5/5` in the still-expanded Approval artifact. Both light/dark cases reproduced before repair: [red log](../evidence/round-3-design-ux/proof-red.log). The open proof now refreshes when its score/iteration changes. Effect cleanup rejects older responses, including a deliberately held pre-rating response delivered after the current evidence. Failed proof reads remain inline with shared `LoadState` and keyboard Retry; they no longer collapse the evidence and leave only a toast.

Acceptance: after rating 5→1 the open Approval says 1/5, never reverts when a held old response arrives, and a failed read recovers in place. **Passed.** Real commands are harmless `true` fixtures whose labels exercise the actual command/proof pipeline; no real provider receives a message.

**R3-DUX-2 · medium · fixed: interrupted score publication has no useful recovery and looks like a zero score.** `ui/src/modules/skills-eval/RunDetail.svelte:491`, `:497`, `:621`. The Round 2 pending snapshot was visible as a zero iteration badge, an old best-score summary, and an unexplained Pending pill. The recovered durable rating could not clearly be distinguished from failed scoring. [Red log](../evidence/round-3-design-ux/pending-red.log). Pending iterations now use neutral pending badges; the stale summary is suppressed while a score is pending; the retained calculated composite is explicitly provisional. A shared inline error explains that the rating is saved and offers Retry to complete publication. Retry preserves the existing rating note and uses the already guarded rating path.

Acceptance: injected publication failure clears winner action, shows the saved rating plus pending/provisional labels, hides the old best summary, and offers a keyboard-accessible retry; recovery restores the real passing proof. Phone controls remain in the viewport with no horizontal page overflow. **Passed.** The storage failure/pending GET is route-injected to make this presentation deterministic; this browser test does not claim to reproduce the backend storage fault. The independent correctness reviewer owns real persistence-fault coverage.

**R3-UX-3 · medium · fixed: switching workspace from a Proof deep link strands a populated detail pane.** `ui/src/modules/proof/ProofPage.svelte:105`. Auto-selection marked the newly selected workspace complete while the old workspace's URL was being cleared. That URL was read untracked, so after it cleared the new list remained loaded but the detail stayed empty. The actual workspace switcher reproduced `Proof packs` instead of the new pack title: [red log](../evidence/round-3-design-ux/evaluator-final-complete.log). Selection now waits for matching store scope, observes the route, and marks completion after a pack is selected. This also preserves initial deep links and explicit last selection.

Acceptance: switch from workspace A's open pack to B through the real menu; B's existing pack opens, A's title/URL disappear. Initial delayed-bootstrap deep link and remembered selection continue to work. **3/3 passed** after this repair: [log](../evidence/round-3-design-ux/proof-switch-green.log), [results](../evidence/round-3-design-ux/proof-switch-green-results.json).
