## School composition result

Freshly compiled production SPA mounted in the actual child WKWebView, using Apple GPU/WebGL 2 and the production Blender assets (no missing assets). Door pointer handlers entered the fixture classroom. Opening/cancelling the actual Add Workspace modal drove three native hide/resume cycles. This limits the reliability assessment; no repeated-until-green soak is claimed. Original failed and final successful logs are both durable.
