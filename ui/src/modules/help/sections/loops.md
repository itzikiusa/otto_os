## What it's for

Goal loops are now **goals** in Workflows: a goal runs as a workflow you watch on the run page, with its criteria, iterations and budget. This page keeps the loops you ran before that, so their results, criteria and evidence stay readable.

## Getting started

1. Open **Workflows → Runs** and choose **Older goal loops** at the foot of the list (it appears only when you have some).
2. Open a loop to read its iterations, criteria and evidence.
3. To pick one up again, choose **Continue as a goal**. The Goal form opens filled from the loop; start it and it runs as a goal in Workflows.

## Everything it can do

- Lists every goal loop of the workspace, newest first, with its final state.
- Shows each loop's definition, acceptance criteria with their evidence, iterations and changes, read-only.
- **Continue as a goal** starts a new goal from the loop's definition.
- Old links to a loop (`#/loops/…`) still open it here.

## Keyboard shortcuts

None specific to this page.

## Tips and limits

- Nothing here starts, stops or edits a loop any more: start new work with **Run… → A goal…** in Workflows.
