# Room games asset provenance

Every model, texture and sound shipped in `ui/public/room-games/` comes from
Kenney (https://kenney.nl) packs released under **CC0 1.0 Universal**
(https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Each pack's bundled
`License.txt` states "License: (Creative Commons Zero, CC0)". No CC-BY or
unknown-license material is used. Author for all packs: Kenney Vleugels
(Kenney.nl); the Voiceover Pack male voice is by Jeffrey M. Smith, released by
Kenney under the same CC0 licence.

| Pack | Source | License | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mini Characters | https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters | CC0 | `char-*.glb`, `char-anims.glb`, kart drivers, fighter portraits |
| Blaster Kit | https://kenney.nl/assets/blaster-kit | CC0 | shooter weapons, ammo, chests |
| Car Kit | https://kenney.nl/assets/car-kit | CC0 | karts + astronaut drivers, shooter cars |
| Toy Car Kit | https://kenney.nl/assets/toy-car-kit | CC0 | item boxes, bananas, finish gate |

The exact model list, per-model bounds, file sizes and SHA-256 hashes are in
`ui/public/room-games/manifest.json`, generated by `build-kits.mjs` from the
lists in `sources.mjs`. Portraits in `ui/public/room-games/portraits/` are
renders of those CC0 models made with Blender by `render_portraits.py`; road,
curb, checker and other textures are generated at runtime in code.
