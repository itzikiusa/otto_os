## Excluded from the commercial Mac path

Keep the working `models/`, `engines/`, virtual environments, download caches and `out/` out of the Forge source distribution. Some software and models may lawfully be repackaged with their respective terms and notices. The following alternatives have material restrictions:

- The audited NVIDIA nvdiffrast and nvdiffrec revisions restrict ordinary third-party use to research/evaluation without direct or indirect monetary gain. BRIA RMBG-1.4/2.0's public terms require a separate commercial grant for self-hosted commercial use. A game studio needs rights to use those tools in production even if it ships only assets. The selected Metal renderer and BiRefNet matte avoid these dependencies. [nvdiffrast §3.3](https://github.com/NVlabs/nvdiffrast/blob/253ac4fcea7de5f396371124af597e6cc957bfae/LICENSE.txt) · [nvdiffrec §3.3](https://github.com/NVlabs/nvdiffrec/blob/abf3a34b1eb6e782abffefc2462c7e9bcd89f9bb/LICENSE.txt) · [BRIA 1.4](https://huggingface.co/briaai/RMBG-1.4) · [BRIA 2.0](https://huggingface.co/briaai/RMBG-2.0)
- Hunyuan3D-2.1, Hunyuan3D-Omni and HY-Motion have Tencent community licences. Under the audited texts, §5(c) limits use/display/distribution of outputs outside the territory, which excludes the EU, UK and South Korea. §4 requires a separate grant above the 1 million MAU threshold measured across the licensee's products in the calendar month before the relevant release. Those terms can affect a shipped mesh or baked clip. No Hunyuan or HY-Motion weights are used in this path. [2.1 terms](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1/blob/82920d643c0dc2f7bfd7255f45f62d386edfe60c/LICENSE) · [Omni terms](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Omni/blob/4d47c0cc2bd0c4281963a7314ab330a5af36bfa8/License.txt) · [HY-Motion terms](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/HY-Motion-1.0/blob/c71ea6c694d8f221c742ff435d9447fa88f91707/License.txt)

[UAL 1](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary.html) · [UAL 2](https://quaternius.com/packs/universalanimationlibrary2.html) · [Kenney](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters) · [KayKit](https://kaylousberg.com/game-assets/character-animations)

## Fit stage (forge-fit)

`forge/fit/` contains no third-party code or data. It runs inside Blender (GPL-3.0, used as a tool; outputs are not covered by the GPL) and reuses Forge's own animation-library modules (`forge/animlib/retarget.py` joint map, importer and key writer; `forge/animlib/grounding.py` sole sampler and grounding) by import. The bundled interaction templates (`forge/fit/templates/*.json`) and the procedural test props and humanoid (`tests/fit/make_fixtures.py`) are original to this repository. A fitted GLB inherits the licences of its inputs: the character, the props and any clips layered from the animation library keep their own notices, which the fit manifest copies from the inputs' manifests when they exist.
